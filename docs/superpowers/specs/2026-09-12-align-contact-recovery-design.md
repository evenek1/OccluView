## Product contract

1. Showing contacts paints the measured contact colours over each scan's existing appearance. It must not make the scan white. Blue contact marks should read clearly without washing out the underlying scan or inventing a new lighting path. The controls live in a compact horizontal strip inside the 3D viewport, keep their close action visible, and never overlap the Layers overlay or leave the viewport. Details remain available. The close mark is a drawn icon, independent of installed fonts.
2. Best Fit Matching primarily registers a prepared scan against the pre-preparation model of the same jaw. Most anatomy may differ; a small set of unchanged, spatially informative patches may be the only rigid evidence. The solver must search beyond the local ICP radius, reject distractor patches, and only commit a pose and fresh deviation heatmap when the unchanged regions determine it. A refused or unfinished fit must explain why and clear any stale map. The Align panel should use the application's control language and retain Details. Response time and correctness must be measured on distinct real prep/original scan pairs; rigidly displaced copies of one STL are only a baseline.
3. Edit Mesh operations must act on every layer with visible selected faces. Sculpt remains tied to one explicitly selected surface because a brush hit belongs to one mesh. Its cursor appears on the first valid hover after the tool becomes ready.
4. Shortcut help is reachable from Settings without taking toolbar space. The obsolete recent-file-count setting is absent from the UI. UI text must be reviewed in context, with translations kept in sync; a global Humanizer pass is not considered done just because one catalog was edited.
5. Results of background loading must not overwrite edits made after a Replace was authorized. Repeated Replace requests must not run unbounded decoders. The memory-mapped import path needs a defensible file-mutation contract before it can be treated as safe. These are separate engineering tasks from contact/Align UX.

## Current evidence and design

The current contact strip is horizontal (`app_contact_bar.rs`), but its old fixed 300-point left offset overlaps a wide Layers overlay and can leave a narrow viewport. It draws `✕` as text while the application already vendors a Lucide `x` SVG. The strip will compute its position from the same Layers geometry used by the overlay, occupy the top beside it when there is room, and sit below it otherwise. The pointer-ownership check uses that same rectangle only while contacts are open.

The current Align path separates point-pair fitting, ICP refinement and deviation measurement (`app_align.rs`, `align_worker.rs`, `occluview-align`). Historical commits corrected several refusal conditions. Open3D and PCL explicitly separate feature-based global registration of partial surfaces from local ICP. Audit the existing coarse hypotheses and candidate ranking against the prep/original case before changing thresholds. A derived partial/deformed fixture can expose regressions but cannot replace an independent clinical pair. When the unchanged patches do not determine a unique rigid transform, Best Fit must refuse rather than manufacture a confident answer.

The owner clarified that this is pre-preparation versus prepared geometry. A large contact fraction is therefore the wrong objective: preparation can change most of the surface while preserving a few crowns or gingival patches. The implementation samples the actual triangle surfaces, compares local normal histograms (FPFH), requires feature matches to agree on one spatially extended rigid transform, then refines only that proposal with the existing surface ICP. It measures overlap at the proposed pose to lower the trim fraction when needed. A near-seated pair stays on the fast local path. The new global path does not introduce a separate display or measurement authority.

Primary references consulted: [Open3D global registration](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/pipelines/global_registration.html) distinguishes global FPFH/RANSAC from local ICP; [PCL SampleConsensusPrerejective](https://pointclouds.org/documentation/classpcl_1_1_sample_consensus_prerejective.html) validates a similar prerejection pattern; [SlicerAutomatedDentalTools](https://github.com/DCBIA-OrthoLab/SlicerAutomatedDentalTools) gives a domain-specific longitudinal intraoral-scan workflow; [exocad Align Meshes](https://wiki.exocad.com/wiki/index.php/Align_Meshes) documents the dental operator flow; and [CAD_CAM_Messung](https://github.com/DigitaleAesthetischeZahnmedizin/CAD_CAM_Messung) provides a public original/preparation STL example. The adapted histogram method is credited in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-NATIVE.md`; Open3D is not a shipped runtime dependency.

Technical evidence: the public distinct prep/original pair reached the same pose from a near start and a 25 mm translation plus small rotation in about 3.1 seconds each on the quieter test run; the median accepted surface gap was 0.031 mm. A controlled arch with most teeth warped and an unmodified region occupying about one quarter of its width had a maximum known-pose error of 0.006 mm at three probes after the change; the old coarse path returned the wrong pose by 3.897 mm at the original probe while reporting trust. A different upper/lower pair was refused at starts from 0 to 40 mm. These are engineering fixtures, not clinical validation; the owner's own prep/original scans and visual heatmap still decide case acceptance.

Limit: a synthetic arch with only a single 12%-width unchanged strip, and a harder two-edge-patch construction, did not determine a reliable global pose with this feature route. Lowering the feature-support threshold appeared to help near that one strip but exposed a 3.821 mm error at the far side of the arch while reporting trust, so it was rejected. The strict candidate threshold is retained; this release must not claim arbitrary tiny-overlap success. A real extreme prep/original pair and clinical review are needed to guide further work without inventing certainty.
